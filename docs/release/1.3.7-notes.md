# VRMC 1.3.7 — bow in the left hand, reliable short pinch, resolution recovery

Operator test of 1.3.6 (2026-11-12). Installed on D1 as 1.3.7; these fixes are host-tested only.

| Report | Root cause (device log 2026-11-12) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The bow should be in the left hand while it is selected | Vivecraft always draws the main-hand item at its main (right) controller, so the bow sat in the right hand, the draw hand at the cheek. | While a bow is the selected main-hand item, the two hand poses are swapped: the bow shows in, and aims from, the left hand. A mirrored draw (right hand in front) keeps the bow in the right hand. Tune `bow.hand=right` turns this off; `bow.roll.left` (degrees) adjusts the bow's roll in the left hand. While the bow is shown in the left hand, the wrist hotbar follows Vivecraft's off-hand to the **right** wrist; touch it with the left index finger. |
| The hand blocks my view | The bow and the draw hand were drawn at the cheek, right in front of the eye. | The bow is in the front hand now, and the floating first-person hands are hidden only while drawing. |
| No crosshair | The crosshair setting is ALWAYS, but the aim line ran from the eye through the bow-hand wrist, and the occluded crosshair sat directly behind the bow and hand. | The aim line runs over the top of the bow fist (like sighting along the arrow). While drawing, the crosshair is drawn over the bow. Both view settings are restored when the draw ends. |
| Short pinch to place starts breaking with the fingers apart | A pinch was released only once thumb and index were more than 4.5 cm apart. Relaxed fingers after a pinch often rest 2–4 cm apart, so the pinch stayed held past the place window and became a break. | Relative release: a pinch ends once the fingers open 1.5 cm past the closest point of that pinch (never below 3.0 cm, never above 4.5 cm). The place/break split moved from 320 to 380 ms. |
| (found) Low resolution for the whole session | World loading (22 ms frames on the placeholder 1024×1024 target) drove auto scale 0.50→0.35 in the first 10 s. Frames are paced to the 90 Hz display (interval p50 11.10–11.14 ms), so the "headroom" test (p50 < 9.4 ms) could never pass, and it never climbed back in 22 minutes. | No adaptation until the world has been stable for 600 frames. Raises are judged on render work time (VR poll → endFrame, p95 < 70 % of the budget). After a struggling window, the scale may not climb back above that level for about 3 minutes, so it cannot oscillate. `render state` logs now include `busyP50/busyP95/ceiling`. |
